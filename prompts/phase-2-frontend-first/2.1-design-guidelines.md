## 4. Decisions & Assumptions
- **Source of Truth:** `design/design-system.png` is the absolute source of truth for all visual tokens and UI rules.
- **Font Stack:**
  - Serif Display: `Playfair Display` (Page and section titles).
  - Sans-Serif: `Inter` (Headings, body copy, inputs, buttons, and captions).
- **Primary Brand Color:** Green `#10B981` (Primary 500) paired with tints `#34D399` (400), `#6EE7B7` (300), `#A7F3D0` (200), `#D1FAE5` (100).
- **Neutrals:** Slate palette from Neutral 900 (`#0F172A`) to Neutral 50 (`#FAFAFC`) and White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Base Grid & Spacing:** 4px incremental scale (4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 40px, 48px, 64px).
- **Border Radius:** Default component radius is `12px` (`radius-md`).
- **Target File:** `system_design/DESIGN.md` in the workspace root.

## 6. Requirements
- **Color Palette:** Complete definitions for Primary (100-500) and Neutral (50-900, White) with hex values and usage guidelines.
- **Typography & Scale:** Precise definitions for Display 1 (48/56), Display 2 (36/44), Heading 1 (28/36), Heading 2 (22/30), Heading 3 (18/26), Body Large (16/24), Body (14/20), and Small (12/16) including font family, size, line-height, weight, and purpose.
- **Spacing & Layout:** Base 4px system with standard padding and margins for sections, cards, and navigation.
- **Elevation & Radius:** Definitions for radii (xs 4px, sm 8px, md 12px, lg 16px, xl 24px, full circle) and shadows (sm, md, lg, xl).
- **Component Specifications:**
  - Buttons: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Text (Height 44px, Radius 12px, font styles, hover/disabled states).
  - Inputs & Selects: Search/Text inputs and dropdowns (Height 44px, Radius 12px, Border #E2E8F0, Focus #10B981).
  - Badges & Tags: Video, Lesson, Popular tags with respective background and text colors.
  - Status Indicators: In Progress, Completed, Now Playing, Locked.
  - Progress Bar: Track, fill color, label layout.
  - Cards: Course Card, Video Card, Lesson Card, Resource Card layout and content slots.
  - Navigation & Breadcrumbs: Desktop and mobile responsiveness guidelines.
- **Design Principles:** Document Clarity First, Consistency, Focus & Calm, and Accessibility.
